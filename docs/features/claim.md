# Claim — receive SOL from a link

If someone sent you a Dropsome drop, you do not need a wallet first. Open the claim link, install a wallet, import the temporary recovery phrase, connect your wallet to the page, and the claim happens automatically.

---

## What you need

- a phone or computer with internet
- a Solana wallet app
- the claim link you received

---

## Step by step

### 1. Open the claim link
The page will show the drop amount and your recovery phrase.

### 2. Install a wallet
Use an official wallet app such as:

- **Phantom** — [phantom.app](https://phantom.app)
- **Solflare** — [solflare.com](https://solflare.com)
- **Backpack** — [backpack.app](https://backpack.app)

!!! tip "Only download from the official links"
    Fake wallet apps do exist. Use the wallet provider's official site or app store listing.

### 3. Import the wallet with the recovery phrase
This is the most important step. The claim page gives you a **recovery phrase**. That phrase belongs to a temporary wallet made for this drop.

**You must import that exact wallet.** Do not create a new wallet.

1. Open your wallet app
2. Choose **Import wallet** or **Recover wallet**
3. Enter the recovery phrase exactly as shown
4. Finish setup in the wallet app

!!! warning "Why you should not create a new wallet"
    The funds are locked to one specific wallet address. If you create a new wallet instead of importing the temporary one, the drop will not be there.

After importing, connect the wallet on the claim page. The claim happens automatically once the correct wallet is connected — no button to click. The transaction usually completes in a few seconds.

### 5. Your SOL is now in your wallet
Once confirmed, the SOL appears in the wallet you imported. You can hold it, send it, or move it to another wallet later.

---

## Recommended: move funds to your own wallet

The imported wallet was created for the drop. For best security, create a fresh wallet and move the funds there:

1. Create a new wallet in your app and save its recovery phrase securely
2. Send the claimed SOL from the temporary wallet to your new wallet address
3. Delete the temporary wallet if you no longer need it

This keeps your long-term funds in a wallet you control and manage yourself.

---

## What is a recovery phrase?

A **recovery phrase** is a list of 12–24 words that acts like the master key to a wallet. Anyone who has it can control that wallet.

- wallet apps use this standard format
- the phrase on the claim page is the only way to access the drop wallet
- never share it with anyone, even the sender

---

## Safety checklist

- [ ] Downloaded the wallet from the official app/site only
- [ ] Imported the wallet using the recovery phrase from the claim page
- [ ] Confirmed the SOL appeared in the wallet after automatic claim
- [ ] Moved funds to a wallet you control long-term
- [ ] Deleted or archived the claim link after claiming
- [ ] Never shared the recovery phrase with anyone

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely cause | Fix |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| "Wallet not found" | Wrong phrase entered or a new wallet was created instead of importing | Re-import the exact recovery phrase from the claim page |
| "Already claimed" | Someone else used the link | Contact the sender; they may still be able to refund if it is still unclaimed |
| Network error / timeout | Solana congestion or RPC issue | Wait a minute and retry; check [Solana status](https://solana.com/status) |
| "Invalid recovery phrase" | Typo, wrong order, or extra spaces | Copy and paste the phrase carefully and use the exact order shown |
